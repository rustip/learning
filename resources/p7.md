## Unit 2: Distributed Systems Principles

Understanding the fundamentals of distributed systems and algorithms is critical for understanding later topics. [Distributed Systems - Martin Kleppmann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UEAMfLPZZhE&list=PLeKd45zvjcDFUEv_ohr_HdUFe97RItdiB) is an excellent short course that covers the essentials well.

### Lab

Complete the exercises in [Distributed Systems in Rust](https://github.com/pingcap/talent-plan/tree/master/courses/dss) using related videos from [MIT Distributed Systems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cQP8WApzIQQ&list=PLrw6a1wE39_tb2fErI4-WkMbsvGQk9_UB).

## Unit 3: Applied Distributed Systems

- Review distributed systems concepts with [Why Distributed Systems Are Hard](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9GP7MNbaRc) and get an overview of concepts in applied distributed systems by reading the second chapter of [Designing Distributed Systems](https://www.amazon.com/Designing-Distributed-Systems-Paradigms-Kubernetes/dp/1098156358).

- Understand fundamental principles in applied distributed systems with [The 3 Golden Rules of Distributed Systems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yebUpcBTdtQ) and then read at least the first four chapters of [Building Resilient Distributed Systems](https://www.amazon.com/Building-Resilient-Distributed-Systems-Practices/dp/1098163540). I highly recommend reading every chapter of this book.

- Read at least chapters 8, 9, 11, and 12 of [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://www.amazon.com/Designing-Data-Intensive-Applications-Reliable-Maintainable/dp/1098119061). Refer back to the other chapters where necessary. You will learn some of the topics in more detail in future parts.

### Lab

Solve the [Fly.io distributed systems challenges](https://fly.io/dist-sys) using [Solving distributed systems challenges in Rust](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gboGyccRVXI).
