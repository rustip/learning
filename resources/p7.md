# Part 7: Distributed Systems

## Unit 1: Rust Concurrency

Read chapters 15 and 16 of the Rust book.

### Lab

Implement the Rust web server from chapter 21. Complete the Rustlings exercises. Write integration tests using [reqwest](https://docs.rs/reqwest/latest/reqwest).

## Unit 2: Distributed Systems Principles

Understanding the fundamentals of distributed systems and algorithms is critical for understanding later topics. [Distributed Systems - Martin Kleppmann](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UEAMfLPZZhE&list=PLeKd45zvjcDFUEv_ohr_HdUFe97RItdiB) is an excellent short course that covers the essentials well.

### Lab

Complete the exercises in [Distributed Systems in Rust](https://github.com/pingcap/talent-plan/tree/master/courses/dss) using related videos from [MIT Distributed Systems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cQP8WApzIQQ&list=PLrw6a1wE39_tb2fErI4-WkMbsvGQk9_UB). If the PingCAP exercises are too difficult, implement the algorithms you learned in Kleppmann's course in Rust. The MIT videos should also be helpful.

### References

- [Distributed Systems](https://www.amazon.com/Distributed-Systems-Maarten-van-Steen/dp/9081540637)


## Unit 3: Applied Distributed Systems

- [Fallacies of Distributed Systems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8fRzZtJ_SLk&list=PL1DZqeVwRLnD3EjyciYAO82dT9Owiq8I5)
- Review distributed systems concepts with [Why Distributed Systems Are Hard](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9GP7MNbaRc).
- Understand fundamental principles in applied distributed systems with [The 3 Golden Rules of Distributed Systems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yebUpcBTdtQ).
- Read at least the first four chapters of [Building Resilient Distributed Systems](https://www.amazon.com/Building-Resilient-Distributed-Systems-Practices/dp/1098163540). I highly recommend reading every chapter of this book.
- Read at least chapters 8, 9, 11, and 12 of [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://www.amazon.com/Designing-Data-Intensive-Applications-Reliable-Maintainable/dp/1098119061). Refer back to the other chapters where necessary.

### Lab

Solve the [Fly.io distributed systems challenges](https://fly.io/dist-sys) using [Solving distributed systems challenges in Rust](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gboGyccRVXI).
